How to sustain QA job in AI world

  1. Shift from repetitive execution to risk-based thinking and test strategy.
  2. Learn how to use AI tools to improve productivity without depending on them blindly.
  3. Strengthen business domain knowledge so you validate the product from a user-impact viewpoint.
  4. Master exploratory testing, edge cases, and real-world user journeys.
  5. Build strong API, database, and integration testing skills.
  6. Focus on testing the critical path, not just chasing every low-value scenario.
  7. Improve automation skills beyond record-and-playback to framework and architecture understanding.
  8. Develop communication skills to explain defects, risks, and release confidence clearly.
  9. Gain knowledge in security, performance, accessibility, and compliance testing.
  10. Become the QA who connects product risk, quality data, and engineering decisions.
