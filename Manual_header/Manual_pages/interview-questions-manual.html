Interview Questions - Manual

Core manual testing questions with concise answer points to help structure an interview response.

Foundations

  1. What is the difference between verification and validation?
    Verification checks work products against specifications; validation checks that the delivered product meets user needs.
  2. What is the difference between severity and priority?
    Severity describes the impact of a defect. Priority describes how soon it should be addressed.
  3. What is regression testing?
    Retesting relevant existing behavior after a change to detect unintended side effects.
  4. What is the difference between retesting and regression testing?
    Retesting confirms a specific defect fix. Regression testing checks surrounding and existing behavior for breakage.

Test design and delivery

  1. How do you design tests when requirements are unclear?
    Identify the ambiguity and risk, ask stakeholders for a decision, document assumptions, and avoid treating guesses as approved behavior.
  2. How do you prioritize testing with limited time?
    Use impact, likelihood, change size, usage frequency, and dependencies to focus on high-risk paths, then report untested scope.
  3. What makes a good bug report?
    A concise title, reproducible steps, expected and actual results, environment, impact, and relevant sanitized evidence.
  4. When should testing stop?
    When agreed exit criteria are met and remaining risks, defects, and untested areas are understood by decision-makers.
