Test Review Chart

A consistent review path helps teams find gaps before test cases are approved and executed.

Review flow

  1. Ready for review: confirm the case has an owner, requirement link, scope, and test data.
  2. Check coverage: compare scenarios against acceptance criteria, risks, roles, and error paths.
  3. Check quality: confirm steps are unambiguous, one behavior is tested, and expected results are verifiable.
  4. Record findings: leave specific comments tied to a step or requirement; assign an owner and due date for changes.
  5. Resolve and re-review: verify updates and close each finding with evidence or rationale.
  6. Approve or return: approve cases that meet the entry criteria; otherwise return them with clear actions.

Decision checklist

  • Coverage: requirements and high-risk behavior have suitable cases.
  • Clarity: another tester can follow the steps without guessing.
  • Expected results: outcomes specify what should be seen or recorded.
  • Maintainability: cases avoid unnecessary duplication and unstable implementation details.
  • Traceability: each case links to a requirement, risk, or documented exploratory charter.

Review outcomes

Use Approved when entry criteria are met, Changes requested when actionable updates remain, and Blocked when a dependency or requirement decision prevents review.
